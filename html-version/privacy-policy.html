2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information You Provide

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Request a quote, consultation, or site survey
  • Contact us by phone, email, WhatsApp, or website form
  • Engage us to perform construction or property management services
  • Subscribe to updates or download resources

This information may include:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and postal address
  • Property details, project requirements, and budgets
  • Billing details and payment information
  • Any other information you choose to share with us

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address, browser type, and device information
  • Pages visited, referring URLs, and time spent on the site
  • Technical information about how you interact with our site

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from third-party platforms (such as Bark.com) when you engage with our profiles or leave reviews. This may include your name, contact details, and enquiry information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide quotations, consultations, and professional services
  • Communicate with you regarding your project or enquiry
  • Manage projects, suppliers, and subcontractors
  • Process payments and maintain financial records
  • Send service updates and marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Evaluate service performance and improve our website
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations
  • Protect our business, staff, and customers from fraud or misuse

4. Legal Bases for Processing (UK GDPR)

We process personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Contract: Processing necessary to perform the contract we have with you.
  • Consent: Where you have agreed to specific uses, such as receiving marketing.
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with UK tax, accounting, and regulatory requirements.
  • Legitimate Interests: To operate, develop, and promote our business in a way that does not override your rights.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information with:

  • Qualified subcontractors, suppliers, and professional advisors involved in your project
  • Property management partners when providing guaranteed rent or landlord services
  • Technology providers (hosting, email, CRM, payment processors) who support our operations
  • Regulators, law enforcement, or government bodies when required by law

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

6. International Transfers

Our core systems are hosted within the UK or European Economic Area (EEA). If data is transferred outside the UK/EEA, we ensure adequate safeguards are in place such as Standard Contractual Clauses or UK International Data Transfer Agreements.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Project documentation: 6–12 years, depending on building regulations and warranty obligations
  • Financial records: 6 years, in line with HMRC requirements
  • Marketing permissions: Until you withdraw consent
  • Website analytics: Up to 26 months

When data is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymised.

  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent

To exercise your rights, please contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling the request.
