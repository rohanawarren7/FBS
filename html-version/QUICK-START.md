# 🚀 QUICK START - Deploy to Netlify in 2 Minutes

## The Fastest Way to Get Your Website Live

### Step 1: Download Files
Download all files from the `/app/html-version/` directory to your computer.

### Step 2: Go to Netlify
1. Open your browser and go to: **https://app.netlify.com**
2. Sign up for a free account (or log in if you have one)

### Step 3: Deploy
1. On the Netlify dashboard, look for the **drag and drop area**
2. Drag the entire `html-version` folder onto the drop zone
3. Wait 30 seconds ⏳

### Step 4: Your Website is Live! 🎉
- You'll get a URL like: `https://random-name-123456.netlify.app`
- Click it to view your live website!

### Step 5: Customize Domain (Optional)
1. Click **"Domain settings"**
2. Click **"Change site name"**
3. Enter: `fallow-building-services`
4. New URL: `https://fallow-building-services.netlify.app`

## Need a Custom Domain?
(e.g., www.fallowbuildingservices.co.uk)

1. In Netlify, click **"Add custom domain"**
2. Enter your domain name
3. Follow the DNS instructions provided
4. Wait 24-48 hours for DNS to update
5. Done! Free SSL included.
